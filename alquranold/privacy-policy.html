AlQuran — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-23
App: AlQuran (Android)
Package: com.codeflick.alquranpp.quran.readquran
Developer: Abdul Moiz / CodeFlick

This policy explains what data the AlQuran app collects, where it goes, and what choices you have. The short version: we don't run servers that store your data. Everything you do in the app — your bookmarks, your last-read verse, your settings — lives only on your device. The only data that leaves your phone goes to a small number of carefully-scoped third-party services described below.

1. Information we do not collect

We don't run a backend that stores your data. We have no user accounts, no login system, no email collection, no name collection, no profile, and no analytics that tie any activity to you personally.

We do not collect, store, or sell:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, or camera
  • Your browsing history outside the app
  • Your precise location at any time other than the moment you tap a prayer-time or qibla feature (see §3)
  • Any messages, files, or content from other apps

2. Information stored only on your device

The following data is created by your use of the app and saved locally on your phone using MMKV (an encrypted-at-rest key-value store). It never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it (e.g., the "Share" button on a verse).

  • Onboarding state — whether you've completed the first-run flow
  • Selected language, theme, font, font size, reciter, translation preferences
  • Bookmarks — the verses you've saved
  • Last-read position — which surah and verse you last opened
  • Reading streak — how many consecutive days you've opened the Quran
  • Tasbih counts — your dhikr counters per preset
  • Notification preferences — whether daily ayah and adhan reminders are enabled, and the time you chose for daily ayah
  • Persona — your selection from the onboarding persona picker

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. You can also clear it manually via your device's Settings → Apps → AlQuran → Storage → Clear data.

4. Quran content — Quran.com Foundation API

When you read the Quran in the app, your device makes anonymous HTTPS requests to the public Quran.com Foundation API (api.quran.com) to fetch:

  • The 114 surahs and their metadata
  • The 30 juz and their verse mappings
  • Verses by surah, in Uthmani script
  • Translations (you choose which ones in Settings)
  • Search results when you use the search bar

We do not send any personal information with these requests. The Quran.com Foundation may keep standard web-server access logs (IP address, user-agent, timestamps) as is normal for any internet service. Please refer to quran.com for their handling of those logs.

5. Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version of the app shows ads on support screens only (the Quran-browse list, search, bookmarks, prayer times, tasbih, hijri calendar, dua collection, the "More" tab, and settings). No ads are ever shown inside the Quran reader, the Mushaf page view, the translation reader, the verse action sheet, the audio player (when added), or the Today home screen.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, a service owned by Google LLC. AdMob may collect a limited set of device-level signals to serve relevant ads, including:

  • A reset-able advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID — you can reset or delete it in your device settings)
  • Device type, OS version, language, country code (coarse)
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address (not your precise GPS)

For users in the EEA, UK, and other regions where GDPR or similar laws apply, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent screen on first launch. You can choose to allow personalized ads, allow only non-personalized ads, or refuse entirely. By default, the app requests non-personalized ads.

Google's full AdMob privacy disclosure is at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Audio — when added

When audio recitation is added in a future update, audio files will stream from the public EveryAyah.com / Quran.com CDN as standard HTTPS requests. Same standard server logging applies as in §4. No personal data is attached.

7. Prayer times — local calculation

Prayer times are calculated locally on your device using the adhan library based on your latitude, longitude, and your selected calculation method. No request is sent to compute prayer times.

8. Sharing data — only when you tap "Share"

Tapping the Share button on a verse action sheet triggers Android's native share intent. You then choose where to send the verse (WhatsApp, SMS, email, etc.) and we have no involvement after that point — your chosen app handles the message. We do not see who you shared with or what you sent.

The Copy button puts the verse text on your device clipboard. We do not read your clipboard at any other time.

9. Children's privacy

The app is rated for general audiences and we believe it is appropriate for ages 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The app does not have a "designed for children" classification and Google Play's Families program rules do not apply.

If you are under 13 and believe a parent or guardian has provided data through this app, please contact us using the email below and we'll address any concern, even though by design we hold no personal data to delete.

10. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not store anything on our servers, there is nothing to retain or delete on our side.

Local-only data on your device:

  • Clear data in Android Settings → Apps → AlQuran → Storage → Clear data — wipes bookmarks, settings, streak, last-read, everything.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the app and all its local data.

11. Your rights

Even though we do not have any personal data of yours to manage, you always have the following rights:

  • Right to know what data is processed about you (this policy)
  • Right to control permissions at the OS level — granted permissions can be revoked in Android Settings
  • Right to opt out of personalized advertising — UMP consent on first launch, or device Ad Settings later
  • Right to delete local data — clear app data or uninstall

If you have any question about how the app handles data and want a written response, please email the address below.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially in the future, the in-app About screen will note the new effective date, and the policy URL will reflect the latest text. There is no in-app pop-up because we don't collect contact info to alert you. We encourage you to re-read this page whenever a new app version is released.
