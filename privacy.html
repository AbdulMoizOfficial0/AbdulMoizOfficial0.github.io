Privacy Policy

Quran Majeed: Tilawat & Recite · Last updated: June 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Quran Majeed: Tilawat & Recite ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The App is developed and operated by Qwark.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not require an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number. The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your reading progress (chapters and pages read)
  • Your Tasbeeh counter values
  • Your personal targets and preferences
  • App settings (theme, language)

This data is stored using your device's local storage and never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob in order to keep the App free for users. Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process the following information:

  • Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate geographic location (country / region, derived from IP address)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks)

This information is collected by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and used for purposes including ad personalization, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and security. For more details, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services.

3. Permissions Used by the App

  • Internet — required to load advertisements.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — used by Google AdMob to deliver relevant ads.
  • Notifications — used to send local prayer-time and reading reminders. No notification data is sent to any external server.

4. Your Choices and Consent

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App will show a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you launch it. You can choose to:

  • Consent to personalized ads, or
  • Refuse, in which case only non-personalized ads will be shown.

You may reset advertising-related preferences at any time from your device's system settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Rights of Users in the European Union (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, as well as the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because the App does not directly collect personal data, most of these rights apply only to data processed by Google AdMob. To exercise rights against Google, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Rights of Users in California and Other US States (CCPA / State Privacy Laws)

If you are a resident of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, or another US state with applicable privacy laws, you have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. The App does not sell personal information. You may opt out of personalized ads through your device's system settings as described in Section 4.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below and we will take steps to remove it.

8. Data Security

Because the App does not transmit personal data to our servers, the primary security measure is the security of your own device. We recommend keeping your operating system updated and using a screen lock.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
