# Privacy Policy for Al-Quran _Last updated: 19 June 2026_ Al-Quran ("the App") is an Android application developed by **Abdul Moiz / Codeflick** ("we", "us", or "our") that helps you read, listen to, and reflect on the Holy Qur'an. This privacy policy explains what information the App collects, why, and how it is handled. It applies to the Android app published on Google Play under the package name `com.codeflick.quranapp.quranmajeed`. --- ## TL;DR — privacy by default - **No accounts.** You don't sign up or log in. - **No analytics by default.** We don't track how you use the App. - **Your data stays on your device.** Reading progress, bookmarks, notes, preferences — all stored locally. - **We never sell or share your data with anyone for marketing purposes.** --- ## What the App stores on your device The App stores the following locally on your phone using Android's encrypted storage (MMKV and AsyncStorage). **None of this is sent to us.** - Reading progress (which ayat you've reached in each surah) - Bookmarks you save, with your optional reflection notes - Preferences: chosen reciter, translation, tafsir source, font size, theme, Mushaf mode - Khatam plan settings (30/60/90/365 days) and daily reminder time - Audio playback cache (mp3 files for offline listening, with a configurable size cap) - Streak count and a reading-history list of the last surahs you opened Uninstalling the App deletes all of the above. You can clear the audio cache from the Library → Downloads screen at any time. --- ## What is sent over the internet ### Content fetched from free public services To deliver Quranic content, the App makes standard HTTP requests to: | Service | Purpose | Sent | |---|---|---| | EveryAyah.com | Audio recitation mp3 files | URL of the requested ayah | | Quran.com Foundation API (`api.quran.com`) | Tafsir commentary | URL + ayah reference | | alquran.cloud API | Quran Arabic text + translations | URL + ayah reference | | Tanzil Project CDN | Additional translation downloads | URL of the translation file | | AWS S3 (Mushaf images) | Printed-page view (optional Mushaf Mode) | URL of the page image | These requests carry the standard HTTP information that any browser sends: your IP address, user agent, and the URL being fetched. We don't add any other personal information to these requests. ### Advertising (Google AdMob) The App displays banner and occasional interstitial ads through Google AdMob to keep the App free. When ads are shown, the AdMob SDK may send to Google: - Your **advertising ID** (a non-personal, resettable identifier you control in Android settings) - Approximate location derived from your IP address - Device type and Android version - Whether the App is installed via Play Store In the EU/EEA, UK, and other GDPR regions, on first launch the App shows a consent form (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform — UMP). You can choose to allow personalized ads, decline (and see non-personalized ads), or change your choice later from the AdMob consent screen. Google's use of your advertising ID is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset your advertising ID at any time: **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads** You can opt out of personalized ads entirely: **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID** A future "Remove Ads" in-app purchase will allow you to remove all advertising entirely. --- ## Permissions the App requests | Permission | Why | |---|---| | `INTERNET` + `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Fetch audio, translations, tafsir | | `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` + `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK` | Keep audio playing when the screen is off | | `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (Android 13+) | Show the daily reading reminder you scheduled | | `WAKE_LOCK` | Prevent audio interruption during long recitations | | `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Re-schedule your daily reminder after a device restart | | `VIBRATE` | Vibrate the notification at your chosen time | | `AD_ID` | Google AdMob (see Advertising section above) | The App does **not** request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, phone state, or any other sensitive permission. --- ## Children's privacy The App is appropriate for users of all ages and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has interacted with the App in a way that disclosed personal information, please contact us so we can investigate. --- ## Data retention - **On-device data**: lives until you uninstall the App or clear it from within the App. - **AdMob ad-serving data**: governed by Google's data retention policies. - **Our servers**: we don't operate any. We store no data about you. --- ## Your rights Because the App stores no personal data on our servers: - There is no account to delete. - There is no profile to export. - Uninstalling the App removes everything related to you on your device. To control advertising data, use Android's built-in advertising controls (Settings → Privacy → Ads), or wait for the upcoming "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations are respected through the consent flow described above.
