privacy policy

Al-Quran

Last updated: 19 June 2026

Al-Quran ("the App") is an Android application developed by Qwark ("we", "us", or "our") that helps you read, listen to, and reflect on the Holy Qur'an.

This privacy policy applies to the Android app published on Google Play under the package name com.codeflick.quranapp.quranmajeed.

TL;DR — privacy by default
  • No accounts — you don't sign up or log in.
  • No analytics by default — we don't track how you use the App.
  • Your data stays on your device — progress, bookmarks, notes, preferences, all local.
  • We never sell or share your data for marketing.

What the App stores on your device

The App stores the following locally on your phone (MMKV and AsyncStorage). None of it is sent to us.

  • Reading progress (which ayat you've reached in each surah)
  • Bookmarks you save, with optional reflection notes
  • Preferences: reciter, translation, tafsir source, font size, theme, Mushaf mode
  • Khatam plan settings and daily reminder time
  • Audio playback cache (mp3 files for offline listening, with a configurable size cap)
  • Streak count and a reading-history list of the last surahs you opened

Uninstalling the App deletes all of the above. You can clear the audio cache from Library → Downloads at any time.

What is sent over the internet

Content fetched from free public services

To deliver Quranic content, the App makes standard HTTP requests to:

ServicePurposeWhat is sent
EveryAyah.comAudio recitation mp3 filesURL of the requested ayah

alquran.cloud APIArabic text + translationsURL + ayah reference

These requests carry the standard HTTP information any browser sends: your IP address, user agent, and the URL being fetched. We don't add any other personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner and occasional interstitial ads through Google AdMob to keep the App free. When ads are shown, the AdMob SDK may send to Google:

  • Your advertising ID — a non-personal, resettable identifier you control in Android settings
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device type and Android version
  • Whether the App is installed via Play Store

In the EU/EEA, UK, and other GDPR regions, the App shows a consent form on first launch (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform — UMP). You can allow personalized ads, decline (and see non-personalized ads), or change your choice later.

Google's use of your advertising ID is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID at any time:

Android Settings → Privacy → Ads

You can opt out of personalized ads entirely:

Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID

A future "Remove Ads" in-app purchase will allow you to remove all advertising.

Children's privacy

The App is appropriate for users of all ages and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Data retention

  • On-device data: lives until you uninstall the App or clear it from within.
  • AdMob ad-serving data: governed by Google's policies.
  • Our servers: we don't operate any. We store no data about you.

Your rights

Because the App stores no personal data on our servers:

  • There is no account to delete.
  • There is no profile to export.
  • Uninstalling the App removes everything related to you on your device.

GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations are respected through the consent flow described above. If you have a specific request, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be announced in the App's "What's new" screen on the next update. Continued use after a change means you accept the new policy.
