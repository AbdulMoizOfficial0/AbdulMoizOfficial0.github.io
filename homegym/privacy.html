Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-05

1. Introduction

HomeGym: Workout Plan ("the App", "we", "our") is a free Android fitness application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and your rights.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. What We Collect and Store

2.1 Data stored locally on your device only

The App stores the following on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party:

  • Your selected workout preferences (goal, equipment, fitness level)
  • Your workout history (dates, durations, exercises performed, sets and reps)
  • Your streak count
  • Your notification reminder time and settings
  • Your unit preference (imperial or metric)
  • Your favorite programs and exercises

You can wipe all locally stored data at any time from Profile → Settings → Clear all data.

2.2 Advertising data collected by Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. Through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (AAID)
  • Approximate (non-precise) location, derived from IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • Timing of ad views and interactions
  • Basic app-usage signals used to select which ads to serve

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Google may share aggregated advertising signals with advertisers.

2.3 What we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Your photos, videos, contacts, or files
  • Precise (GPS) location
  • Health or biometric data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Any account or login credentials — the App has no login system

3. How the Information Is Used

  • Locally stored data powers the App's features (streak tracking, workout history, personalization). It is used entirely on your device.
  • Advertising data collected via AdMob is used to serve ads and measure ad performance. Advertising is how the App remains free.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — ad serving. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK — technical library that displays the ads. Same policy.

The App does not integrate general-purpose analytics services (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics), crash reporting SDKs, or attribution services that transmit personal data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

Google AdMob, as an advertising network, may share aggregated advertising signals with advertisers to serve relevant ads. Refer to Google's policy for details.

6. Data Security

Locally stored data is protected by the standard Android app sandbox — no other app on your device can read it. Data transmitted by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit by Google.

7. Data Retention

  • Locally stored data is kept on your device until you clear it via Settings or uninstall the App.
  • Advertising data collected by Google is retained per Google's policy.

8. Your Rights and Controls

  • Delete all local data at any time via Profile → Settings → Clear all data.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all local data.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads personalization".

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since the App has no server-side account system, there is no personal data on our servers to delete.

10. International Users

The App is available worldwide via the Google Play Store. Where required by law (including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation and California Consumer Privacy Act), the App presents an in-app consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for personalized advertising.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Effective date" at the top will change. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
